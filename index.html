How to Calculate UP Board Percentage

UP Board calculates scores based on each subject carrying a maximum of 100 marks (including theory and practical/internal assessment components).

Percentage (%) = (Total Marks Obtained / Maximum Marks) × 100

Passing Criteria: A student is required to secure at least 33% marks in each subject to pass the examination.
